4. Environmental Ethics and Legal Practice

Environmental responsibility: Protect people and property while reducing harm to marine habitats, fisheries and beaches.

Fairness: Consider local residents, fishing communities and future generations.

Professional duty: Engineers must use evidence, disclose uncertainty and avoid misleading safety claims.

Legal responsibility: Real coastal projects must check applicable environmental permits, coastal regulations and local authority requirements. In India, the Coastal Regulation Zone framework may be relevant depending on the site and activity.

5. About the Methods

Seawall: Hard structure that can shield land from wave action, but may affect beaches and nearby erosion.

Breakwater: Offshore barrier that reduces incoming wave energy, but may change sediment movement.

Mangroves: Nature-based protection that can support habitats, but effectiveness depends on location and conditions.

Multi-functional approach: Compare safety, habitat, community needs and environmental impact rather than choosing only the strongest wall.
